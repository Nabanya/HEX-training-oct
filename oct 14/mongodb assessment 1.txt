85. Explain briefly why you used:
an array for categories - It allows storing multiple category values per supplies within a
single field for straightforward multi value matching.
example:A single supplier can sell goods in more than one category (e.g., both "Electronics" and "Accessories").

an embedded document for contact details - Contact info consists of multiple related details (like phone number and email) 
that always belong together as a single package.Putting them inside a nested document keeps the data neat, organized 
and tied directly to that specific supplier.

an array for warehouse locations - One supplier can have multiple warehouse locations across different cities.
Storing them in a list (["Banjara Hills", "Hitech City"]) inside the suppliers document
means MongoDB can retrieve the supplier and all their warehouse locations instantly in a single lookup without needing extra collections or complex code.
